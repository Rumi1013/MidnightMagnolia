The work between sessions

You have the language. You have the frameworks. You are still stuck in the middle of yourself.

You have done the therapy. You have read the books. You can name a thought distortion before it finishes forming. You know the words attachment, regulation, somatic, lineage, generational.

And still — the deepest thing is not yet examined. The pattern you keep returning to is not yet understood. The piece that was passed down has not been separated from the piece that is yours. You are not at the beginning anymore. But you are not yet on the other side.

The wellness industry will sell you another twelve-week intensive. Your therapist's calendar is full until November. The journals at the bookstore are paced for someone in their first season of healing. You are not in your first season. You are in the season where the work becomes more honest, and there is almost nothing built for it.

What this is

A guided system for the woman already in the work.

Fourteen weeks of structured examination, organized as a spiral rather than a staircase — returning more honestly each pass, never demanding you arrive somewhere.

The Deep Roots Shadow Work System is not a starter journal. It assumes you have done the surface work and are ready to look at the layer underneath — the patterns you carry that were never only yours, the inheritances you have not yet named, the cost-benefit math of behaviors you have been calling problems when they were actually old protections.

It does not promise breakthroughs. It promises honest inventory — the slow work of looking at what you carry with enough love to look clearly, and enough rigor to set down what is finally ready to be set down.

About the work
