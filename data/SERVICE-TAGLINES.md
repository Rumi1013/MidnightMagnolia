Voice anchors: Audre Lorde clarity, Bernice McFadden tenderness, Beyoncé restraint. No "transform your life" or hustle language. Always meet the reader where they are.

## How to apply these

1. Open **Wix Bookings → Services**
2. For each of the 5 services above, open the service editor
3. Paste the **Primary** tagline into the "Tagline" / "Subtitle" field (Wix calls it `tagLine` in the API)
4. Save — within ~60 seconds, your Next.js `/services` page will pick it up via Incremental Static Regeneration

| Specific outcomes | Vague aspiration |
