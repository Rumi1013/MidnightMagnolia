⚠️ COMMON QUESTIONS

Q: Will it delete my files automatically?
A: No. Nothing happens without your approval. You review and click "Delete".

Q: What if I accidentally delete something?
A: Files go to Mac Trash first. You have 30 days to recover them.

Q: How long does a scan take?
A: First scan: 5-20 minutes (depends on file count)
   Later scans: 2-5 minutes (only new/changed files)

Q: Can it break my projects?
A: No. The system knows about projects and keeps them together.

Q: Does it work without the dashboard?
A: Yes! Scanner works standalone. Just run the Python script.

Q: Can I schedule this?
A: Yes! Dashboard settings let you set weekly scans (default: Thursday 10PM)
