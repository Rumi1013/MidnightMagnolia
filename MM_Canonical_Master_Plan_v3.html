Voice Rules — Non-Negotiable
  • Healing-centered, not performative. The voice holds space; it does not command.
  • Southern Gothic register: literary, place-rooted, unafraid of shadow and grief.
  • Anti-hustle always. Banned words: grind, crush it, kill it, scale fast, optimize, leverage, hustle.
  • Neurodivergent-affirming: gentle pacing, one visible next action, no walls of text.
  • Trauma-informed: no urgency language, no countdown timers, no manufactured scarcity.
  • Lowcountry-rooted: Latisha's voice, story, and Vincent family genealogy are the brand differentiator — no one else has this.
  • Recovery-aware: never shame or pathologize. The brand lives in reclamation, not diagnosis.
  • CTAs are invitations: "Begin when you're ready." not "Buy now!"
  • Never open with "I" — open with the reader's experience or a grounded observation.
  • Consistent softness creates sustainable income. Speed is never the goal.
