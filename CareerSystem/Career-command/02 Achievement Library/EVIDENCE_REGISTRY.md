# Career Command Evidence Registry

Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

This registry points to source material without copying it. It is intentionally limited to career-relevant work and excludes identity, medical, financial-account, background-check, legal-case, and unrelated personal records.

## Evidence rules

- **Third-party verification** can support a factual claim when the source clearly names the person, program, award, grade, or result.
- **Authored work sample** proves capability and scope, but does not by itself prove that every planned activity or target was completed.
- **Self-reported outcome** may support interview preparation, but a numeric résumé claim remains unverified until corroborated.
- **Public** means suitable for a portfolio after a final rights/privacy check. **Excerpt only** means redact participant, partner, funder, legal, or internal details. **Interview only** means do not publish.

## Thin or missing proof to seek next

1. Independent records supporting the current résumé metrics for total funding, participant counts, files digitized, people trained, testing seasons, programs delivered, and audit findings.
2. Completion evidence for planned work: launch records, dated deliverables, attendance summaries, evaluations, before/after measures, board minutes, funder acceptance, or supervisor/client confirmation.
3. Clear attribution on shared curriculum, presentations, guides, and marketing assets: creator credit, revision history, assignment rubric, email acceptance, or testimonial.
4. Outcome evidence for implementation work: adoption rate, time saved, error reduction, reporting cycle improvement, user count, completion rate, or stakeholder satisfaction.
5. ATS scoring is still pending because a defensible score must be calculated against a specific job posting and a specific résumé version; it is not a universal résumé grade.

## Excluded from the career evidence bank

Credit reports, payment exports, police or court records, complaints, identity documents, medical records, background-check data, participant case files, passwords, and unrelated personal material are excluded even if discovered during the scan.
