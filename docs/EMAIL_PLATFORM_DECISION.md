# Email platform (blocking item)

The Master Plan marks **email platform choice** as blocking for: lead magnet delivery, Dusk Letters at scale, and affiliate traffic follow-up.

## Shortlist (pick one, then wire)

| Platform | Pros | Watchouts |
|----------|------|-------------|
| **ConvertKit** | Creator-focused sequences, tags, landing pages | Paid tiers scale with subscribers |
| **Mailchimp** | Familiar, generous free tier early on | Heavier UI; deliverability tuning |
| **Stan native** | Single stack with Stan Store | Feature depth vs dedicated ESP |

## Definition of done

1. Chosen ESP + account on brand domain (or verified sending domain).
2. One **welcome sequence** (Master Plan: ~3 emails) for Stan purchasers / lead magnet — even if manual trigger at first.
3. **Next step in repo:** replace “email capture above fold” (`site-email` task) with a real form or ESP embed (Wix or Next).

This file is a decision log only — no automation ships until the above is true.
