# Publication policy

- Submissions enter a pending state and are not public automatically.
- Human review is required at meaningful approval points.
- Public responses exclude internal safety notes, reviewer identity, private contact details, credentials, and production secrets.
- Resource-search behavior is not marketing consent.
- Living-person genealogy data defaults to private or restricted unless permission and purpose support publication.
- Facts, probable claims, conflicting claims, and unresolved hypotheses remain distinguishable.
