# Classification and retention

## Classification
- Tier 1: Confidential or restricted.
- Tier 2: Internal.
- Tier 3: Public.

Every record receives a classification, retention category, responsible owner, and review date at intake.

## Illustrative retention categories
- Grant and funder compliance records: seven years past grant close-out, or longer when required.
- Personnel records: seven years past separation.
- Board minutes and governance records: permanent.
- Participant or client intake records: applicable program requirement, with a minimum three years past last service date in the source framework.
- Routine correspondence and internal drafts: two years, then disposition review.

## Legal hold
When litigation, audit, or investigation is reasonably anticipated, affected retention timers are suspended. Destruction of Tier 1 records requires documented approval.
