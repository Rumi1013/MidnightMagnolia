Brand Voice Rules
Voice Principles always apply
  • Healing-centered, not performative. The voice holds space; it does not command action.
  • Southern Gothic register: literary, rooted in place, not afraid of shadow or grief.
  • Anti-hustle always. Never use words: grind, crush, kill it, scale fast, optimize, leverage.
  • Neurodivergent-affirming: gentle pacing, one visible next action, no walls of text.
  • Trauma-informed: no urgency language, no countdown timers, no manufactured scarcity.
  • Lowcountry-rooted: Latisha's voice, story, and genealogy are the brand differentiator.
  • Recovery-aware: never shame or pathologize. The brand lives in reclamation, not diagnosis.
  • Preferred sentence length: medium. Two short sentences over one run-on.
  • Never open with "I" — open with the reader's experience or a grounded observation.
  • Calls to action are invitations, not commands: "Begin when you're ready." not "Buy now!"
